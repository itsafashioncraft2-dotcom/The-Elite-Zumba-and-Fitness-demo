FAQ

Got questions?

Where is the studio located?
We are at Kotwali Chowk, Dr. R.P. Road, in front of Dr. Aziz Ahmed Clinic, Bhagalpur, Bihar 812002. Use the “Get directions” button in the Contact section to open it in Google Maps.
What are your opening hours?
We are open all 7 days, from 6 am to 11 pm.
Which programs do you offer?
Zumba, yoga, and weight, strength and cardio sections, plus a steam bath and free pre-workout. Message us on WhatsApp if you would like to know what suits your goals.
Do you have Zumba sessions? How do I join?
Yes, Zumba is one of our core programs. Tap “Join a Zumba session” to message us on WhatsApp and ask for the available timings and details.
I am new to fitness. Can I still join?
Members often mention our supportive trainers who guide them on form and technique. Message us on WhatsApp and tell us where you are starting from, and we will guide you.
What are the membership plans and fees?
Monthly ₹2,500, Quarterly ₹7,500, Half-Yearly ₹10,000 and Yearly ₹14,000. See the Membership section above, or message us on WhatsApp for the latest details.
How can I contact the studio?
You can chat with us on WhatsApp or call 090655 16492.
